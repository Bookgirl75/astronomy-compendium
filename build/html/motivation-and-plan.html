Motivation and plan

My motivation: collecting my knowledge and sharing it with others in an open and accessible format.

Your motivation: you are fascinated by astronomy and would like to learn more. There is very much jargon and a huge backlog of knowledge, so we are trying to give you the minimum you need to start learning specific parts of the field or looking at a data set.

We will introduce you to the bare minimum of concepts you need from physics and chemistry, as well as telling you some of the background on how astronomers have put together their picture of stars and the universe.

Our hope is that you will read this in a fraction of a day and then tell yourself “now I can talk to astronomers without both of us getting frustrated”.
